# Generic agent instructions

## Slop prevention

When answering a question, always clearly distinguish which of your findings are *fact-based* (for example, you have found specific code that proves them, or you have tested them) from those that are *assumptions* (based on your own knowledge rather than something you verified). This rule should not reduce the amount of information you provide or require you to find proof for everything; its only goal is to inform the user about your level of confidence.

## Language

Use American English – color, behavior, initialization, etc.
